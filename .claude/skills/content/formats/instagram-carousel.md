# Instagram carousel

Where it goes: Instagram feed as a multi image post. Slides are designed in the site look. This file gives the text of every slide, the design notes, and the caption.

Limits
- 6 to 10 slides. Instagram allows 20. Nobody reads 20.
- Slide size 1080 by 1350 (4:5). Keep text inside a 920 by 1190 safe area.
- Slide text: headline under 8 words, body under 25 words. One idea per slide.
- Caption under 2,200 characters. Only the first 125 characters show before "more". Aim for 300 to 800 characters.
- No hashtags. No emoji. Link in bio only, so the caption says "Link in bio" or "marinlazic.com/assessment" in words.

Shape
- Slide 1: the hook. The claim in under 8 words, big. Nothing else except the Marin Lazic wordmark small at the foot.
- Slide 2: the problem or the moment. Why this matters to someone who runs things.
- Middle slides: one point per slide. Mechanism, then proof, then what to do. Each slide a headline plus one or two lines.
- Second last slide: the action for this week.
- Last slide: the call to action. "Take the free assessment. 5 minutes. marinlazic.com/assessment" or the one from the core. Wordmark.

Design notes (from the site)
- Ivory paper #FAF9F5. Ink type #141413. Body #3D3D3A. One signal #FF5A36 element per slide at most: a short rule, one word, a number.
- DM Sans bold for headlines, tight tracking. Inter for body. Sentence case.
- Alternate: text slides on ivory, and one or two of Marin's black and white photos full bleed with a short line on an oat #F0EEE6 card.
- Slide numbers small at the foot, "3 / 8".
- No icons, no gradients, no stock.

Caption
1. First line repeats the hook or gives the one line the slides build to. Under 125 characters.
2. Two to five short lines of the mechanism or the proof, for people who read captions.
3. The action.
4. The call to action in words.

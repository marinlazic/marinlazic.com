# YouTube Short

Where it goes: YouTube, uploaded as a vertical video under 60 seconds. Marin talks to camera. This file is the script, the on-screen text and the metadata.

Limits
- 30 to 50 seconds. Under 60 is the rule. At talking pace that is 75 to 125 spoken words.
- Vertical 9:16, 1080 by 1920. Keep text out of the bottom 25 percent and the right edge, where YouTube puts its controls.
- Title under 80 characters, sentence case. Description under 300 characters. No hashtags. Do not add "#Shorts"; YouTube detects the format.

Shape
- 0 to 2 seconds: the hook, spoken, with the same words on screen. The claim or the moment. No "Hey everyone".
- 2 to 10 seconds: why this matters to someone who runs things.
- 10 to 35 seconds: the mechanism and the proof. One fact on record.
- 35 to 45 seconds: the action for this week. One thing.
- Last 3 to 5 seconds: the call to action spoken as a plain sentence. "The free assessment is at marinlazic.com." Nothing else.

Writing notes
- Spoken language. Contractions. Sentences under 10 words. Nothing that needs a comma to say.
- Write it as Marin talks, not as he writes. Read it aloud. If a line is hard to say, change it.
- One take, one location, no cuts needed. Suggest a shot: Marin at the pool deck, the gym, or his desk, with a single line of what is in frame.
- On-screen text: 3 to 6 captions, each under 6 words, sentence case, placed in the upper 60 percent. Mark where each appears.
- No music suggestion. Marin's voice carries it. Auto captions on.

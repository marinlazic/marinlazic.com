# X article

Where it goes: X, "Write article" (Premium long form). Rendered with headings and images, lives on the profile.

Limits
- 600 to 1,200 words. X readers are in a feed. Shorter than LinkedIn.
- Title under 60 characters, sentence case.
- Cover image 1600 by 900. Marin's photo or an ivory card with the title.

Shape
1. Title: the claim.
2. First paragraph: the claim and the stakes, 2 to 3 sentences. Readers arrived from a short post and want the long version now.
3. Three or four H2 sections, headings as statements. Shorter sections than LinkedIn, 80 to 200 words each.
4. One short list of what to do this week, 3 to 5 lines.
5. Call to action with the link, one paragraph.

Writing notes
- More direct than LinkedIn. X rewards the blunt version. Still no hype, still no exclamation marks.
- Fewer qualifiers. Say the thing, then the limit in one sentence.
- Link the article from the last post of the X thread.
- The LinkedIn article and the X article share facts and structure but are not the same text. Cut, do not copy.

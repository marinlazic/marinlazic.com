# Instagram Reel

Where it goes: Instagram Reels. Vertical video, Marin to camera. Usually the same footage as the YouTube Short, re-cut and re-captioned for Instagram. This file gives what changes.

Limits
- 20 to 45 seconds. Reels can run longer. Short ones get finished and shared.
- Vertical 9:16, 1080 by 1920. Keep text inside the middle 60 percent of the height: Instagram covers the bottom with the caption and the right with buttons.
- Cover: a frame or a card with the hook in under 6 words, in the site look. Give the words.
- Caption under 2,200 characters, aim for 150 to 500. First 125 characters show before "more". No hashtags. No emoji. "Link in bio" or the address in words.

Shape
- Same five beats as the Short: hook in 2 seconds, why it matters, mechanism and proof, action, one plain sentence of call to action.
- Trim the Short script by 10 to 20 percent. Cut the second half of the mechanism first.
- The spoken call to action says "the link is in my bio", not the address, because Instagram does not link captions.

Writing notes
- Spoken language, as for the Short. Reuse the Short's lines where they fit. Do not make a new script if the Short script cuts cleanly.
- On-screen captions: 3 to 5, under 6 words each, sentence case, in the safe area. Instagram auto captions can be on as well.
- Caption: first line is the hook in writing. Then 2 to 4 short lines that add one thing the video did not say. Then the action. Then the call to action in words.
- Original audio only. No trending sound on a talking head.

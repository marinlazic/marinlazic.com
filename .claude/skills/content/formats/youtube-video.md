# YouTube video

Where it goes: YouTube, a horizontal video of 6 to 10 minutes. Marin talks to camera, with cutaways where the script says. This file is the script, the chapter list, the thumbnail text and the metadata.

Limits
- 6 to 10 minutes. 900 to 1,500 spoken words.
- Horizontal 16:9, 1920 by 1080.
- Title under 60 characters, sentence case, the claim or the question the video answers. No clickbait, no caps, no numbers unless the number is the point.
- Thumbnail: Marin's photo or a plain ivory card, 3 to 5 words in DM Sans bold, one signal accent. Give the words.
- Description: first 150 characters carry the claim and the link (they show before "more"). Then chapters with timestamps. Then two links at most. No hashtags.

Shape
1. 0:00 to 0:20. The hook. The claim, and what the viewer will be able to do by the end. No intro music, no "Welcome back".
2. 0:20 to 1:00. Who Marin is, in two sentences, as the reason to trust the claim. Then the plan of the video in one sentence.
3. Chapters. Three to five, each 1 to 2 minutes, each with a sentence case chapter title that is a statement. In each: the point, the mechanism, the proof on record, what to do. Mark one cutaway per chapter (a drawing of the training curve, a shot from the pool, a screen of the assessment) in square brackets as a direction, not spoken.
4. Last chapter, about 1 minute: what to do this week. Three steps, spoken slowly, each repeated on screen.
5. Close, 20 to 30 seconds: the call to action as a plain sentence, then stop. No "like and subscribe" speech. One sentence is fine: "If this is useful, the free assessment takes 5 minutes and the link is below."

Writing notes
- Spoken language throughout. Shorter sentences than the articles. One idea per breath.
- Directions in square brackets, on their own line, never spoken.
- Each chapter must be cuttable as its own Short. Write the first line of each chapter so it works cold.
- Facts on record only. Where a story would help and none is on record, `[MARIN: tell the story of ...]` with what kind of story.
- Give the chapter list with timestamps estimated from word count at 150 words a minute.

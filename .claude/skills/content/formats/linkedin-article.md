# LinkedIn article

Where it goes: LinkedIn, "Write article". Long form, lives on Marin's profile, indexed by search.

Limits
- 800 to 1,500 words. Longer only if the idea needs it.
- Headline under 60 characters, sentence case. It is the only thing most people see in the feed.
- Cover image 1920 by 1080. Use one of Marin's black and white photos or a plain ivory card with the headline.

Shape
1. Headline: the claim, plain. Not a question, not "How to" unless it is how to.
2. First paragraph: 2 to 3 sentences. The claim and why the reader should care this week. No throat clearing.
3. Three to five sections with H2 headings in sentence case. Each heading is a statement, not a label ("Rest is where the gain happens", not "Recovery").
4. In each section: the point, the mechanism, one example or fact from the record, what to do.
5. A short "What to do this week" section with 3 to 5 numbered steps. Each step one line.
6. Close with the call to action, one paragraph, with the link written out. Links are fine inside articles.

Writing notes
- Paragraphs of 2 to 4 sentences. Short lines between them are fine.
- Bold is allowed for one phrase per section at most. No italics for emphasis.
- Pull quotes: one, if there is a line worth it. Marin's own line, not someone else's.
- Credentials once, early, as a fact that earns the claim. Then stop.
- Link to the matching article on marinlazic.com/insights if one exists, in the body, where it helps.

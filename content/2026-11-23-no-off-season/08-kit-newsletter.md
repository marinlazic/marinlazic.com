Hi {{ subscriber.first_name | default: "there" }},

Last week the team finished a block and stopped. Not because anyone was tired. Because the plan said so. The rest was in the diary before the block began.

You don't get that. Your year runs straight through. So when you build a training plan you build it in a quiet week, for a quiet week, and it lasts about three weeks before work gets loud and it's gone.

The plan was never too hard. It was built for the wrong week.

Training is a stress. You get fitter afterwards, while you repair, and only if you keep repeating the loop. One big week followed by three empty ones does less than four modest weeks in a row.

So build it the other way round. Think of your worst week this year. Count the sessions you still did. That number is your floor. Put that many in the diary as fixed, strength first. Everything above it is a bonus. In a loud week, drop back to the floor and feel nothing about it.

Then find the heaviest fortnight in your next quarter and book an easy training week in front of it. We do this before every tournament. Nobody calls it slacking.

Note 1 (the claim)

You don't get an off season.

Athletes do. Build, peak, recover, then stop. The rest is in the plan before the season starts.

Your year never stops. So your training plan can't be built for a quiet week. It has to fit inside the week you actually have.

Note 2 (the proof)

Four campaigns with the Australian men's water polo team since 2023. Every one built, peaked and recovered to a plan.

Nobody trained flat out all year. Not because they couldn't. Because flat out all year is how you arrive fried.

Most founders train flat out for three weeks and then not at all. Same mistake, shorter cycle.

Note 3 (the action)

Look at your worst week this year. Count the sessions you still did.

That's your floor. Build the plan on that number, not on your best week.

Everything above it is a bonus. Two sessions you keep for a year beat five you keep for three weeks.

# You don't get an off season

Athletes get a season. You get a year, and all of it is heavy. That is why your training plan is gone by week three, and it is also how to build one that lasts.

I coach strength and conditioning for the Australian Men's Water Polo Team. Four campaigns since 2023, Paris 2024 among them, and every one had the same shape. Build, peak, recover. Load up in steps, easy weeks planned in, rest booked before the season starts. Nobody trained flat out all year.

A founder has none of that. No tournament to peak for, no end of season to rest in. So the structure that a season gives an athlete has to live somewhere else. It has to live inside your week.

## The plan is built for the wrong week

You decide to get serious. You build a five session week, and you can do it, because you built it on a quiet Sunday. Three weeks in, a deal closes or a flight does. You miss two sessions, then four. By the end of the month the plan is something you used to do.

The plan was not too hard. It was built for your best week. You get a handful of those a year.

## The gain is in the repeat

Training is a stress. You do not get fitter during the session. You get fitter afterwards, while the body repairs and builds a little extra. Work, rest, adapt, go again from a higher base.

That loop only pays if it keeps running. One big week and three empty ones does less than four modest weeks in a row. The whole reason a season has easy weeks in it is to protect the repeat. You cannot protect it with a season, so protect it inside the week.

## Find your floor

Think of the worst week you have had this year. Travel, deadline, sick kid. How many sessions did you still do? That number is your floor. It is what your life carries when everything goes wrong, and it is what the plan should be built on.

Put that many in the diary as fixed. Strength first, then 30 to 40 minutes with your heart rate up. Choose the days you are most likely to be home and the time you are least likely to be interrupted.

Everything above the floor is a bonus. Quiet week, add one. Loud week, drop back and feel nothing about it. You did not fail the plan. The plan was built for this.

## Book the easy week first

Teams take an easy week before a tournament block, not after the fatigue shows up. Once it shows up, the choice has been made for you. Find the heaviest fortnight in your next quarter and put an easy training week in front of it. Keep the floor, drop the weights by a third, go to bed early.

## This week

1. Write down your worst week and the sessions you kept. That is your floor.
2. Put the floor in the diary as fixed, strength first.
3. Decide now what you add in a quiet week.
4. Book an easy week before the next heavy fortnight.

Two sessions you keep for a year beat five you keep for three weeks.

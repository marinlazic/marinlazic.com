You don't get an off season.

My athletes do. Every campaign since 2023 has been built, peaked and recovered to a plan. Load goes up in steps. Easy weeks go in before the hard ones. Time off goes in the diary before the season starts.

Then the season ends, and they rest.

Your year does not end. Q4 runs into January. The raise runs into the hire. So you do what most busy people do. You build a training plan for a quiet week, run it for three weeks, and lose it the first time work gets loud.

The plan was never wrong. It was built for the wrong week.

Training is a stress. The body gets fitter afterwards, while it repairs. The result comes from repeating that for months, not from one big week. A plan you can only keep when work is quiet breaks that loop every time work is not.

So build it the other way round.

Look at your worst week this year. Count the sessions you still did. That is your floor. Put that many in the diary as fixed. Everything above it is a bonus, not a failure when it goes.

Then book one easy week before your next heavy quarter. Teams do this before every tournament. Nobody calls it slacking.

Two sessions you keep for a year beat five you keep for three weeks.

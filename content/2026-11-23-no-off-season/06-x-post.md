## Thread

1. You don't get an off season. That one fact explains why most training plans for founders are gone by week three, and how to build one that lasts the year.
(154)

2. Training is a stress. You don't get fitter in the session. You get fitter afterwards, while you repair. Work, rest, adapt, go again. The gain comes from keeping that loop running for months.
(190)

3. Athletes protect the loop with a season. Build, peak, recover. Four campaigns with the Australian men's water polo team since 2023, every one to a plan, with the easy weeks booked before the hard ones.
(201)

4. You have no season. So you build the plan in a quiet week, for a quiet week. Five sessions. It works until work gets loud, and then it's gone. The plan wasn't too hard. It was built for the wrong week.
(201)

5. Find your worst week this year. Count the sessions you still did. That's your floor. Put that many in the diary as fixed. Add in quiet weeks, drop back in loud ones, feel nothing about it.
(188)

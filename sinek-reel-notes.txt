SIMON SINEK REEL ANALYSIS
=========================
Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DVGoej5CinN/
Caption: "Our passion lies deep in who we are, not what we do."
Context: Video from Chick-fil-A Next 2025, in conversation with Chief Legal Officer Lynette Smith

KEY MESSAGE:
- Passion is rooted in identity (WHO you are), not activity (WHAT you do)
- This is classic Sinek "Start With Why" philosophy applied to personal identity
- The distinction between being driven by purpose vs. being defined by job title

SINEK'S CORE PHILOSOPHY (for bio rewrite):
1. START WITH WHY - Lead with purpose, not credentials
2. People don't buy WHAT you do, they buy WHY you do it
3. The Golden Circle: Why → How → What (most people go outside-in, leaders go inside-out)
4. Passion comes from identity, not from activity
5. Great leaders inspire action by communicating their beliefs first
6. Finite vs Infinite Game - play the long game, not the short one

APPLICATION TO MARIN'S BIO:
- Current bio leads with WHAT (S&C Coach, credentials, certifications)
- Sinek approach: Lead with WHY Marin does this work
- What drives him? What belief system underpins his coaching?
- The bio should make someone feel something, not just list achievements
- Identity > Title: Who is Marin at his core, beyond the job description?

# LinkedIn About Me — Marin Lazic

---

I coach humans to perform. Olympians, execs, and dads who are one Demon Hunters dance session away from needing a sports physio.

Here's the thing nobody in this industry wants to say out loud: emotions drive performance more than any program ever will. Reps and sets are cute. But if your stoke tank is empty, none of it matters.

Quick story. Four weeks before a World Championship, I got dropped from my national team. Went from the fittest I'd ever been to not being able to make warm-up times. You don't lose your fitness overnight. That's not how biology works. What I lost was something deeper. I grew up with Eastern European parents where stress was basically a swear word. You don't talk about it. You just push through and eat more cabbage rolls. That moment broke me. But it taught me the most important lesson of my career: the body doesn't lie. Stress isn't weakness. It's biology.

Now I lead Strength and Conditioning for the Australian Men's Water Polo Team. Paris 2024 Olympics. World Championships. The LA28 pathway. My job is simple. Make sure the boys are stronger, faster, and still standing when everyone else is wrecked. Bend without breaking.

I started Otion to take this beyond sport. Olympic-level coaching for executives and organisations. Because the biology of performance doesn't care if you're in a pool or a boardroom. High performance isn't about medals. It's about consistency, recovery, and not falling apart under pressure. Olympic athletes need to get ready. Corporate athletes need to stay ready.

2025/26 Update:
• Preparing the Aussie Men's Water Polo Team for the LA28 podium
• Boosting corporate clients' HRV, VO₂ max, and grip strength so they can be fire, not a candle
• Growing Otion because most corporate wellness programs are still serving vibes instead of results

Look, I'm just a guy who selfishly loves helping people because it fills my own stoke tank. And oh yeah, I also happen to know how to coach Olympians. Niiice.

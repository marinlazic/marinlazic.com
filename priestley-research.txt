DANIEL PRIESTLEY FRAMEWORKS - RESEARCH NOTES
=============================================

1. THE 7-11-4 RULE (from Oversubscribed)
- 7 hours of content consumption needed to move from stranger to trust
- 11 interactions/touchpoints (emails, videos, social posts, etc.)
- 4 different platforms/locations (website, social, email, events)
- Goal: turn strangers into warm leads before any sales conversation

2. SCORECARD MARKETING (ScoreApp)
- Interactive quiz/assessment as lead magnet
- Prospect answers questions about their situation
- Gets personalized results/score
- You get qualified lead data (knows their pain points, readiness level)
- Much higher conversion than PDF lead magnets (40-60% completion vs 5-10% for PDFs)
- Priestley co-founded ScoreApp specifically for this

3. OVERSUBSCRIBED CAMPAIGN (5 Phases)
- Planning: Know capacity, who it's for, when you deliver
- Build-up: Signal to market, get them to signal back intention to buy
- Release: When oversubscribed, release in stages/price points
- Remarkable Delivery: Under-promise, over-deliver
- Innovate & Celebrate: Share success stories, analyze data

4. KEY PERSON OF INFLUENCE (5 Steps)
- Pitch: Perfect your message
- Publish: Write a book or create signature content
- Product: Productize your expertise
- Profile: Raise your visibility
- Partnership: Strategic alliances

5. LAPS FRAMEWORK (from 24 Assets)
- Lead: Generate leads through content and lead magnets
- Appointment: Convert leads to conversations
- Presentation: Present your solution
- Sale: Close the deal

6. 24 ASSETS (7 Groups)
- Intellectual Property: Content, Methodology, Registered IP
- Brand: Philosophy, Identity, Ambassadors
- Market: Positioning, Channel, Data
- Sales: Lead Generation, Nurturing, Conversion
- Product: Core Offering, Productized Service, Joint Ventures
- Systems: Automation, Reporting, Delivery
- Culture: Team, Values, Community

7. KEY PRIESTLEY PRINCIPLES
- "People don't buy what others want to sell. They buy what others want to buy."
- Create supply-demand tension (limited capacity)
- Be remarkable so customers create the "sizzle"
- Talk about something bigger than what you do
- Nurture existing clients, let them pull a crowd
- Share client success stories to attract aspirational prospects
- Use technology for automation and scaling remarkable delivery
